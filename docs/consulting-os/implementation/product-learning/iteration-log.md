# Product Learning Iteration Log

## 2026-08-15 — Guided engagement records

Feedback: Product, audit, and interview cards looked complete but were not operational entry points. The second consultant/client selector also repeated a choice already made on the landing page.

Learning: In a consulting platform, a register card must open the underlying work, not merely summarize it. First-open guidance should begin with adjacent writable questions, while experienced consultants must retain direct view/edit access. Conversation and native UI should operate on one domain contract so answers do not diverge across surfaces.

Regression rule: Role-specific entry links must never present a second role selector. Product, audit, and interview cards must remain keyboard-accessible controls that open their typed guided record.
